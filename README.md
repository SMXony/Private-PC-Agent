# Private PC Agent
An AI that lives on YOUR PC. No cloud. No tracking. No monthly fee.

**Status:** Building in public from Dhaka, Bangladesh. v0.1 - Research & Roadmap phase.

### Why?
Every AI today sends your chats, photos, and files to someone else's server. Students, journalists, and normal people in low-income countries cannot afford privacy.

Private PC Agent will be 100% free and open-source. Anyone can download it, use it offline, and modify it. No account. No subscription. No data leaves your room.

### Who is it for?
- Students who cannot afford ChatGPT Plus
- People who want private AI without internet
- Anyone who does not want Big Tech to read their chats

### How will it work? (v1 Plan)
1. Runs 100% offline on your own PC
2. Uses small open-source models (no API key needed)
3. Reads local files only if you allow it
4. No data ever sent to cloud
